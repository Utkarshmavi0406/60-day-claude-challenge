## My Startup Idea: AI-First Schools India

> **The vision:** A school where AI education is mandatory from Class 5, equipping Indian students to intern at companies while still in school — years before they reach college.

**Founder:** Utkarsh Mavi

### 14. Go / No-Go Recommendation
> **Score: 67/100 — CONDITIONAL GO ✅**

The problem is real. The timing is right. The founder's conviction is credible.

But the physical school model is a 10-year capital-intensive bet. **Start with an after-school program or curriculum SaaS.** Reach 200 paying students. Prove outcome metrics (projects built, internship placements, parent NPS). Then raise capital for the school.

**The vision is fundable. The current execution model is not.**

## Key Learnings

1. **A great problem + wrong execution model = slow death.** The physical school vision is right but the first step should be an after-school program or curriculum SaaS — not an application for CBSE affiliation.

2. **The teacher supply problem is actually the product.** Whoever solves "how do we get qualified AI teachers into 1.5M Indian schools" wins the whole market. That itself could be a ₹1,000 Cr business.

3. **The internship pipeline is the real moat.** No edtech has wired curriculum → project → portfolio → internship → income. That full loop is worth owning.

4. **Validate 4 assumptions before spending a rupee on infrastructure:**
   - Will parents pay an AI premium?
   - Will schools partner?
   - Can Class 5 students build real AI projects?
   - Will companies give micro-internships to school students?

5. **The founder's personal story IS the pitch.** A Credit Risk Analyst using AI daily who wishes he'd learned it at 10 to help his family financially — that's a compelling founder narrative that any impact investor will find credible.
