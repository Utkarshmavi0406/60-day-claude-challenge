About Me

I am a data scientist specialising in credit risk analytics, currently pursuing my MS in Business Analytics and AI at UT Dallas. Before that, I spent two years at Bajaj Finance building production-grade PD scorecards and NLP pipelines that influenced lending decisions for thousands of borrowers.
