## Key Learnings

**1. A toolkit beats a resume.** A resume answers one question: what have you done? A toolkit answers every question a recruiter, hiring manager, or referral contact might have — before they ask. Building it forces you to think about your career from every angle simultaneously.

**2. Personalization at the message level is where most candidates fail.** Generic outreach gets ignored. The difference between a recruiter email that gets a reply and one that doesn't is almost always specificity — a real number, a real role, a real company name. Claude makes personalization fast enough to do it at scale.

**3. Skill gaps split into two categories and the split matters.** Vocabulary gaps close with a rewrite. Real skill gaps close with learning. Treating them the same wastes time on the wrong fix. The roadmap only works when you know which type of gap you are actually closing.

**4. Your 30-second intro is your most used career asset.** You will use it more than your resume — at networking events, career fairs, LinkedIn voice notes, Zoom intros, and every cold call. Writing it down and practicing it is not optional at the senior level.

**5. Personal branding is positioning, not marketing.** The goal is not to sound impressive. The goal is to make it immediately obvious to the right person why they should talk to you specifically. The positioning statement does that — it answers "why this person, for this role, right now" in three sentences.
