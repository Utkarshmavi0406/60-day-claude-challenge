Summary
Leadership reviewed Q2 performance against the annual growth target. Revenue grew 12% versus an 18% target, driven primarily by enterprise deals slipping into next quarter (9 of 14 expected contracts closed, due to procurement delays and additional client-requested security reviews). Marketing reported a 34% increase in website traffic but lower-than-expected conversion rates, prompting a planned website redesign. Engineering capacity emerged as a recurring constraint across multiple initiatives — the August analytics dashboard release, a possible website redesign, and ongoing reporting-performance issues affecting both customer support tickets and sales prospects. Customer retention remains strong at 94%, though support ticket volume has increased significantly. Several decisions were explicitly postponed: hiring headcount (until Q3 forecasts are finalized), the customer conference budget (pending vendor proposals), and any public commitment to the August dashboard release date (pending an engineering milestone review). Open items will be revisited at next week's leadership meeting.
