## Key Insight: The Dual Customer Problem

AIcademy has TWO customers who require completely different messaging:
- **Student** = end user (experiences the learning)
- **Parent** = buyer (signs the cheque)

You cannot talk to both the same way. Two separate funnels, two separate messages. Market to parents about **career outcomes and AI-proofing**. Market to students about **building cool things**.

## Top 10 Customer Pain Points

1. **The 11-Year Compounding Loss** — AI learning starts at BTech (~21), losing 11 years since Class 5
2. **Income Delay for Middle-Income Families** — Can't afford 2-3 years of upskilling at 21 (founder's own story)
3. **CBSE "AI" is Theory with Zero Practical Output** — Students can define "deep learning" but never ran Python
4. **Parent AI Anxiety is Acute and Unsatisfied** — 71% of Indian parents fear AI will hurt their child's career (EY 2024)
5. **No Qualified AI Teachers Anywhere** — India: 9M teachers, <1% can teach AI
6. **WhiteHat Jr Left a Trust Scar** — Millions paid ₹30-80K, quality collapsed post-BYJU's acquisition
7. **JEE/NEET Fear Blocks "Distractions"** — Must position AI as a JEE accelerator, not a competitor
8. **No Portfolio or Proof of Learning** — Students have no projects, no visible output, no proof
9. **Language & Context Barrier** — Indian students need Indian examples, not Western datasets
10. **No Bridge Between Learning and Earning** — Zero pipeline from school AI skills to real income

## MVP Recommendation

### Build This First: AIcademy Weekend Program — Batch 1

| Spec | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Format | Online (Zoom/Google Meet) |
| Schedule | Saturdays, 10AM–12PM IST |
| Duration | 12 weeks (1 batch = 3 months) |
| Class Size | 15–20 students max |
| Target Age | Class 5–8 (ages 10–13) |
| Instructor | Utkarsh Mavi (the founder) |
| Price | ₹1,499/month × 3 = ₹4,497 total |
| Break-even | 10 students (₹14,990/month) |

### 12-Week Curriculum
- **Weeks 1–3:** What is AI? Prompt engineering, ChatGPT mastery
- **Weeks 4–6:** No-code AI tools (Teachable Machine, Make.com)
- **Weeks 7–9:** Python basics for AI (Google Colab)
- **Weeks 10–12:** Capstone project + Demo Day presentation

### Build First ✅
Curriculum content, landing page (Carrd/Notion), Razorpay link, WhatsApp community, free demo class, Week 12 Demo Day

### Do NOT Build Yet ✗
Mobile app, school B2B infrastructure, teacher training platform, physical classroom, internship pipeline, custom LMS

### The One Metric That Matters
> By Week 12, do 80%+ of students present a working AI project at Demo Day?  
> **YES** → curriculum works → scale it  
> **NO** → fix curriculum before adding a single new student

## Pricing Hypothesis

| Tier | Price | Target |
|------|-------|--------|
| Founder Cohort | ₹999/month | First 10 students — collect testimonials |
| Standard Batch | **₹1,499/month** | Main price point (3-month commit = ₹4,497) |
| B2B School License | ₹300/student/month | Phase 2 — after MVP validation |

**Key insight:** Indian parents apply "price = quality." Do NOT go below ₹999/month or you signal low quality to the exact demographic you need.

Break-even: 10 students at ₹1,499 = ₹14,990/month (achievable with zero marketing spend)

## 30-Day MVP Plan

### Week 1 — Discovery
- Days 1–2: 10 parent problem interviews (not pitches)
- Days 3–4: Build landing page on Carrd with 2-min Loom video
- Days 5–7: Share in 10 Indian parent WhatsApp groups + LinkedIn founder post

### Week 2 — Prototype
- Days 8–10: Design free 60-min demo class (start building in minute 1, no theory first)
- Days 11–12: Set up Razorpay payment link, test full flow
- Days 13–14: Confirm Zoom setup, practice demo once alone

### Week 3 — Validation Sprint
- Day 15: Run Free Demo Class #1 (target: 20+ attendees)
- Days 16–17: Post-demo survey + call every parent who attended
- Days 18–19: Decision gate — 8+ yes → collect payment, 4–7 → run second demo, <4 → fix messaging
- Days 20–21: Build full 12-week curriculum (Weeks 1–4 in detail)

### Week 4 — Launch
- Days 22–24: Confirm enrollments, set up WhatsApp group, send welcome message
- Days 25–27: Register on Startup India (DPIIT recognition, free)
- Days 28–30: Run Batch 1 Week 1 → document everything → post LinkedIn recap

## Key Learnings

1. **The dual customer problem is real and expensive to ignore.** The parent pays, the student attends. Two different funnels, two different messages, two different conversion moments.

2. **The founder IS the product for Batch 1.** Utkarsh's story is more credible than any company branding. One authentic LinkedIn post will outperform ₹20,000 in ads.

3. **The MVP is embarrassingly simple.** Zoom + Razorpay + WhatsApp + Curriculum. That's it. No app, no LMS, no school partnership. Just teach 20 kids on Saturdays and measure whether they build a project at Week 12.

4. **"Don't undercharge" is counter-intuitive but critical.** Indian parents price-signal quality. ₹999/month signals seriousness. ₹299/month signals hobbyist. Price is part of the product.

5. **The Demo Day is the most important product decision.** It's not just a showcase — it's a public commitment that keeps students enrolled through the full 12 weeks AND becomes the marketing event for Batch 2.
