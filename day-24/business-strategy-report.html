01Business Reality Check
// HONEST ASSESSMENT BEFORE STRATEGY
This section prioritises evidence over enthusiasm. Every answer below is based on what has been proven vs. what is assumed. Assumptions are flagged explicitly.
WHO PAYS?
B2C (now): Indian parents in metro cities, ₹1,499/month. They pay because of AI anxiety and FOMO, not because they've seen outcomes. B2B (Phase 2): Private school management committees, ₹300/student/month for curriculum licensing. They pay to satisfy parent demand and NEP 2020 compliance. B2G (Phase 3): State education boards — long sales cycle (18–36 months), but highest scale.
⚠️ Assumption: Parents will pay ₹1,499/month without seeing a track record. This is the #1 assumption to validate via demo class conversion rate.
WHY THEY PAY?
Parents pay because: (1) AI displacement anxiety is acute and real, (2) no credible structured alternative exists for ages 10–13, (3) WhiteHat Jr left a gap in the market, (4) the founder's personal story creates trust that a brand never could.
⚠️ Assumption: The "working AI project in 12 weeks" promise is the primary conversion trigger. If parents care more about CBSE alignment, the messaging needs rework.
HOW DISCOVERED?
Phase 1: Founder LinkedIn post → WhatsApp parent group sharing → Free demo registration. Phase 2: Demo Day videos as UGC → Instagram reels → Batch 2 applications. Phase 3: School principal LinkedIn outreach + EdTech India conferences + Demo Day outcomes data.
⚠️ Assumption: Founder's LinkedIn reach is sufficient for Batch 1 without paid ads. Need minimum 5,000+ LinkedIn connections or 3 viral posts to generate 50+ demo registrations.
GROWTH RISKS?
1. Founder bottleneck — can't teach more than 20 students as sole instructor. 2. No India operational presence — founder based in US (Texas). 3. WhiteHat Jr trust scar — market is sceptical of EdTech promises for kids. 4. JEE/NEET parent mindset — any "distraction" from competitive exams faces automatic rejection. 5. Low LinkedIn reach limits organic Batch 1 discovery.
Mitigation roadmap included in GTM and Action Sheet sections.
MONETISATION RISKS?
1. Indian parents already spend ₹8–15K/month on tuitions — AI program is additive spend, not a switch. Spend fatigue is real. 2. School B2B sales cycle is 12–18 months minimum. Revenue from schools is Year 2+ only. 3. Teacher certification market needs proof of student outcomes first. 4. Price below ₹999/month kills perceived quality; price above ₹2,999 without outcomes kills conversion.
WEAK ASSUMPTIONS?
Weakest → validate urgently:
(1) Parents will convert at 40%+ from demo to paid — test with first demo class
(2) Class 5 students can complete a working AI project in 12 weeks — test with pilot
(3) School principals will respond to cold LinkedIn outreach — test with 20 outreach attempts
(4) The teacher playbook can be built from Batch 1 and used by a co-facilitator — test in Batch 2
(5) Demo Day videos become organic marketing — test with first Demo Day video
