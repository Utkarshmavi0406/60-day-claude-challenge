## Go-To-Market Strategy

**Phase 1 (Months 1-3):** Founder-Led Organic
- LinkedIn founder story → WhatsApp groups → Free demo → Batch 1 enrollment
- CAC: ₹0 | Target: 20 students

**Phase 2 (Months 4-12):** Demo Day UGC Flywheel
- Demo Day video → Instagram Reels + LinkedIn → Batch 2-4 applications
- Each parent refers avg 1.5 new parents (viral coefficient: 0.75)
- CAC: ~₹500 | Target: 100 students

**Phase 3 (Year 2):** B2B School Distribution
- Principal LinkedIn outreach + Demo Day proof + free pilot offer
- CAC: ₹15-30K/school | Target: 3 school pilots → 300-1,500 students/school

**Phase 4 (Year 3):** Teacher Certification as Distribution Engine
- Certified teachers recommend AIcademy to their school and parents
- Each certified teacher = distribution channel

## Final Verdict

**Score: 64/100 — 🟡 VALIDATE**

The business model is coherent, the market is real ($117B India K-12), and the path from ₹3.6L to ₹80M ARR via B2B curriculum SaaS is clear. What's missing is not strategy — it's evidence. Batch 1 completion data, parent testimonials, and one school pilot conversation would move this from 64 to 80+ and make it genuinely investable.

**Sustainability Verdict:** AIcademy India is sustainably viable if the Demo Day flywheel works — one cohort's output becomes the next cohort's marketing, reducing CAC toward zero while revenue compounds. The teacher certification business creates a second revenue stream that controls its own distribution, making the long-term competitive position defensible at scale.

---

## Key Learnings

1. **The Demo Day is the entire growth strategy, not just an event.** One parent-shared video of their child building AI unlocks the next cohort at ₹0 CAC. Every business decision should protect and amplify Demo Day.

2. **Investor Readiness (42/100) is the honest number.** The story is compelling but zero traction means zero investment conversations should happen before Batch 1 ends. This isn't discouraging — it's a 90-day timeline to fundable.

3. **The B2B unlock is the scale lever.** Individual parent acquisition has a ceiling. One school partnership at ₹300/student/month = 500 students = ₹1.5L/month from one deal. B2B changes the economics entirely.

4. **LTV:CAC at 18x is the strongest signal in this whole analysis.** When CAC is near ₹0 (organic LinkedIn) and LTV is ₹8,994 (two batches), the unit economics are exceptional. The only job is to not spend money on paid acquisition before the organic engine is maxed out.

5. **The teacher certification business hiding inside this startup is worth more than the school business.** India has 9 million school teachers who cannot teach AI. Whoever certifies them controls K-12 AI curriculum distribution for the next 20 years.
