Workflow

❓ What is this tool?

The Personal AI Playbook is a reusable system for the AI prompts you actually use repeatedly at work — built around credit risk analysis, SQL/Python query writing, and turning messy findings into committee-ready memos, since that's what eats the most of your time.

🗂 Dashboard — pre-built, editable workflow templates organized into 4 categories relevant to your job. Click any card to fill in its variables and copy a ready prompt.
🧩 Prompt Builder — for anything not covered by a template. Assemble a custom prompt from building blocks (Role, Objective, Context, Constraints, Reasoning Strategy, Output Format, Tone, Examples, Quality Checks) with a live preview as you go.
🔁 Loop Builder — takes any prompt and turns it into an autonomous loop: define a Goal, how to Evaluate output, an Improvement Strategy, Stop Conditions, and Safety Rules, so Claude can self-check and revise its own work.
⭐ My Workflows — anything you've favorited from the Dashboard or saved from the builders lives here, searchable and filterable.

Everything is stored locally in your browser via localStorage — nothing is sent anywhere. Use Export/Import (top right) to back up or move your library.

Keyboard shortcuts: 1-4 switch tabs · / focuses search on Dashboard · Esc closes any open modal.
