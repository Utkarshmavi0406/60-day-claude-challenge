How This Analysis Works

Five specialists, one synthesis — no hardcoded scoring anywhere

Hook Reviewer evaluates only the portion of your post visible before LinkedIn's "see more" truncation — the single highest-leverage few seconds of attention you get. Structure Reviewer checks formatting and scannability for a mobile feed. Engagement Psychology Reviewer looks for the emotional and curiosity triggers that turn a passive scroll into an active comment. CTA Reviewer judges whether your closing line genuinely invites a response or is generic filler. Content Strategist then reads all four reports plus your original post and synthesizes one decisive final report — every score, sentence, and rewrite you see is that round's literal API response, not a template filled in with your inputs.
