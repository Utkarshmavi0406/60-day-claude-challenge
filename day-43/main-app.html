What this workflow does

The recurring process a credit risk analyst runs to monitor an existing loan book for early signs of deterioration

This workflow takes you from raw loan performance data to a committee-ready escalation decision — six stages, each with the specific AI tools, prompts, and pitfalls that matter for that step. It's built for a monthly (or more frequent) portfolio monitoring cadence on a consumer or retail loan book, tracking delinquency bucket migration and default trends to catch deterioration before it shows up in losses.

Why This Workflow Matters

🎯 Catches deterioration before it hits losses
Delinquency trends typically lead charge-offs by 2–4 months. A disciplined monitoring cadence is the earliest lever risk teams have.
🤖 AI compresses the analysis bottleneck, not the judgment
The slow part of this workflow has always been query-writing, narrative synthesis, and memo drafting — exactly where Claude adds the most leverage, while the actual risk judgment stays human.
🔁 It's a loop, not a report
Stage 6 exists specifically to close the feedback loop — checking whether last cycle's actions actually worked is what separates monitoring from box-checking.
